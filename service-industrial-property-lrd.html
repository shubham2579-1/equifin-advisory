Industrial Property LRD

Industrial Property LRD.

Industrial or manufacturing property leased to an operating tenant generates rent that can be discounted for capital, structured around the specific lease and tenant profile involved.

Industrial Rent Discounting  ·  Manufacturing Lease-Backed Capital  ·  Owner Liquidity

Discuss Industrial Property LRD View Lease Rental Discounting

Where This Applies

  • An industrial or manufacturing facility is leased to an operating tenant.
  • The tenant's operating stability affects how comfortably lenders underwrite the rent stream.
  • The owner needs capital against the property without disrupting the existing tenancy.
  • Remaining lease tenor needs to support the LRD facility's repayment structure.

How We Approach It

We assess the industrial tenant's operating stability and the lease's remaining tenor to structure an LRD facility sized against the property's genuine, contracted rent income.
