What each direction trades off

No direction wins on every dimension — each makes a deliberate trade-off in exchange for its strongest quality.

A — Institutional Finance

  • Strongest continuity with the brand already live today — lowest risk if selected.
  • Reads as safely credible rather than distinctive; the most "expected" of the four.
  • Formal grid and bordered stat table suit a bank-adjacent register well.

B — Premium Real-Estate Finance

  • Highest perceived premium feel — Cormorant + terracotta + capital-stack diagram feel genuinely bespoke.
  • Large italic serif and light body weight cost it a little on fast scanning.
  • Most directly speaks the visual language of real-estate developers specifically.

C — Modern Financial Platform

  • Fastest to scan and read — sans-only, tabular numerals, dashboard-style tiles.
  • Strongest single CTA/data pairing in the hero (chart card next to the headline).
  • Flattest register of the four — least "luxury," most "precise."

D — Executive / Boutique Advisory

  • Most restrained and premium-coded through typography alone — no color, no cards, no icons.
  • Understated CTA (underline link, not a filled button) is a deliberate choice, not an oversight — but it is objectively the least visually assertive of the four.
  • Closest register to a law firm or M&A boutique rather than a lender-adjacent advisory.

No recommendation is made here

All four are real, working, responsive prototypes reusing EquiFin's actual services, figures and positioning language — none contain invented claims. The right next step is to review all four live, at both desktop and mobile width, before narrowing down. Once a direction (or a hybrid) is chosen, it can be built out as the production homepage in a separate, deliberate change — nothing here has touched the live site.
