Not every capital requirement can be addressed through a conventional working capital facility or a standard project loan. Acquisitions, promoter stake transactions, refinancing of expensive debt and other strategic situations often require a financing structure built around the specifics of the transaction. The challenge is not simply finding a lender — it is determining the right capital structure, repayment profile, security package and lender universe before approaching the market.

EquiFin Advisory works with established Indian businesses, promoters and institutional stakeholders on complex corporate finance requirements. We help structure and execute debt transactions ranging from acquisition financing and refinancing to structured and subordinated credit — from evaluating the transaction and developing the financing strategy through to lender positioning, due diligence, documentation and final disbursement.

What This Covers

Acquisition Financing — Debt solutions for the acquisition of businesses, operating assets, business divisions or promoter stakes, subject to transaction structure and lending considerations.

Refinancing & Debt Optimisation — Reviewing existing borrowing where a business has outgrown its earlier capital structure or a stronger credit profile creates room to revisit pricing and tenure.

Structured Debt — Advisory on bespoke debt structures for situations where a conventional term loan may not adequately address the commercial requirement.

Subordinated & Growth Capital — Exploring appropriate subordinated or structured credit options for businesses seeking growth capital without immediate equity dilution.

Bridge Financing — Shorter-term financing structures for time-sensitive transactions pending completion of longer-term funding arrangements.

Why Businesses Need It

A strong business can still struggle to raise capital if the proposed financing structure does not align with how lenders assess risk. Before approaching the market, several questions need to be addressed:

  • What is the underlying purpose of the capital?
  • Can the proposed cash flows support the repayment obligation?
  • How does the new debt fit within the existing capital structure?
  • What level of leverage is commercially sustainable?
  • Which lenders are likely to understand the transaction?
  • What security and guarantees may be required?
  • Is the tenure aligned with the life of the underlying asset or transaction?

The answers influence not only whether financing is available, but also the quality of the eventual terms.

Key Structuring Considerations

  • Existing leverage and debt obligations
  • Operating cash flows
  • Debt servicing capacity
  • Promoter contribution
  • Acquisition or transaction rationale
  • Security availability
  • Corporate and personal guarantees
  • Share pledges, where relevant
  • Repayment profile
  • Lender covenants
  • Inter-creditor considerations

The objective is not to maximise debt at any cost. It is to develop a structure the business can support through its operating cycle.

EquiFin Perspective

A corporate financing requirement should not begin with a lender list. The stronger starting point is the transaction itself — why capital is required, how it will be deployed and how it will ultimately be repaid. Once those questions are addressed, lender selection becomes significantly more focused.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is corporate finance advisory?

Corporate finance advisory helps businesses evaluate and structure capital for strategic requirements such as acquisitions, refinancing, expansion and complex debt transactions.

How is corporate finance different from working capital finance?

Working capital finance primarily supports day-to-day operating requirements such as inventory and receivables. Corporate finance generally addresses larger strategic, structural or transaction-specific capital requirements.

Can existing debt be refinanced?

Refinancing may be considered when a company's credit profile, cash flows or financing requirements have changed. Feasibility depends on the existing debt structure, security, repayment performance and prevailing lender appetite.

What is structured debt?

Structured debt refers broadly to financing arrangements tailored around specific transaction characteristics rather than a standard lending product.

What is a typical ticket size for corporate finance transactions?

Requirements vary by transaction, but corporate finance mandates are typically suited to mid-to-large transactions rather than smaller, routine borrowing needs.

Does EquiFin provide loans directly?

No. EquiFin Advisory acts as an advisory and transaction execution partner. We do not act as a lender or extend credit.
