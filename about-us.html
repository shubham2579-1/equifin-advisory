Capital Is More Than Funding. It Is a Strategic Decision.

Businesses do not raise capital in isolation.

A financing decision affects leverage, cash flows, repayment capacity, asset ownership, growth plans and the long-term financial flexibility of the business.

EquiFin Advisory works with businesses to bring greater structure and strategic clarity to these decisions — from understanding the capital requirement to structuring the transaction and navigating institutional execution.

The Firm

Independent Thinking for Complex Capital Decisions.

EquiFin Advisory is a corporate finance and debt advisory firm focused on helping businesses navigate significant capital requirements.

Our work sits at the intersection of business strategy, financial analysis and institutional capital.

We work with promoters, founders, CFOs and senior management teams who are evaluating financing requirements that demand more than simply identifying a lending institution. These situations often require a deeper understanding of:

  • Why capital is required
  • How much capital is appropriate
  • How the transaction should be structured
  • What cash flows support repayment
  • Which capital providers may be relevant
  • How the transaction should be positioned

Our role is to bring these considerations together into a more coherent financing strategy.

Our Perspective

Financing Is Often Treated as a Product Search. We See It as a Transaction.

In many financing processes, the conversation begins too late. The business identifies an amount of capital, selects a financing product and starts approaching institutions. But the more important questions may remain unanswered.

  • Is the capital requirement correctly defined?
  • Is the proposed debt structure aligned with cash flows?
  • Is the repayment profile realistic?
  • Does the transaction make sense from an institutional perspective?
  • Is the business ready for the financing process?

EquiFin was built around the belief that these questions should be addressed before capital execution begins.

The objective is not simply to raise capital. It is to structure capital in a way that makes sense for the underlying business and transaction.

Our View of Capital.

01

Capital Should Follow Business Reality

A capital structure should reflect how the business operates, generates cash and intends to grow. The financing product should not define the business requirement. The business requirement should inform the financing strategy.

02

Structure Matters

The amount of capital is only one part of the transaction. Tenor, repayment, security, leverage, pricing and structural flexibility can materially affect the long-term impact of a financing decision.

03

Lender Perspective Matters

A transaction cannot be structured solely around what the borrower wants. It must also address the institutional logic behind the financing decision. Understanding how capital providers evaluate risk is an important part of transaction preparation.

04

Execution Requires Discipline

Even a well-conceived financing strategy requires careful execution. Information preparation, institutional positioning, stakeholder coordination and transaction progression all influence the path toward closure.

How We Think

We Start Before the Capital Raise.

Our work begins before institutional engagement. Before approaching the market, we seek to understand the underlying transaction context.

This allows the financing conversation to move beyond a simple question of how much capital can be raised? Towards more important questions:

  • What should the capital support?
  • What structure is appropriate?
  • What cash flows support the obligation?
  • What risks need to be addressed?
  • What institutions may have relevant appetite?
  • How should the transaction be positioned?

A better financing process begins with a better understanding of the transaction.
