Lease Rental Discounting

Your Commercial Property Generates Rent. That Rental Stream Can Also Create Financing Capacity.

A stabilised commercial asset with contracted tenants creates more than property value — it creates recurring, visible cash flow. EquiFin helps commercial asset owners structure Lease Rental Discounting around tenant quality, lease tenure and lender underwriting, unlocking capital without an outright sale of the property.

Where This Becomes Relevant

LRD is most useful when a stabilised commercial asset needs to work harder:

  • Unlocking Capital Without a Sale — against a leased office, IT park, retail or industrial asset the owner wants to keep.
  • Refinancing an Existing LRD — rates, tenure or amortisation no longer fit, or new leases have improved the cash-flow case.
  • Multi-Tenant or Mixed-Use Assets — where different lease expiries and tenant profiles need WALE-driven structuring.
  • Industrial & Warehousing Assets — institutional tenants, but location and re-leasing potential weigh heavily too.
  • Upcoming Lease Expiry — good current rent, but a renewal question that has to be built into the financing structure now.

What Lenders Actually Evaluate

The credit committee looks past the property valuation to the lease itself:

  • Tenant Covenant Quality — who is actually paying the rent, and how reliably.
  • Remaining Lease Tenure & WALE — whether the debt tenor is covered by contracted, not assumed, cash flow.
  • Lock-In & Termination Clauses — how much of the rental stream is actually protected against early exit.
  • DSCR — cash available for debt service against scheduled obligations, typically with a cushion above 1.0x on escrowed rentals.
  • Loan-to-Value — financing against property value, alongside the cash-flow case, not instead of it.
  • Vacancy Risk — replacement-tenant potential and re-leasing timelines if the current tenant exits.

How the Financing Is Approached

Two properties can carry near-identical valuations and still have very different financing profiles — one with a strong MNC tenant on a long, well-protected lease, the other with weak, short-tenure tenants and irregular collections. We underwrite across four layers: the asset itself, the tenant generating the rent, the durability of the lease, and whether the resulting cash flow actually covers debt service. Rentals are typically routed through an escrow with a defined payment waterfall, often backed by a tripartite agreement between owner, tenant and lender — documentation that functions as cash-flow control, not just paperwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lease Rental Discounting?

Lease Rental Discounting, or LRD, is a financing structure where a commercial property owner's contracted rental income is evaluated as a source of debt repayment, with the underlying property typically forming part of the security package.

What is WALE?

WALE stands for Weighted Average Lease Expiry. It measures the weighted average remaining lease duration across a property's tenant base and helps assess future rental cash-flow visibility.

What happens if a tenant leaves before the loan is repaid?

Tenant exit can reduce rental cash flow and create vacancy risk. Lenders may evaluate lease expiry, lock-in provisions, replacement demand and debt service resilience while structuring the transaction.

Does EquiFin provide LRD loans directly?

No. EquiFin acts as an independent financial advisory firm supporting transaction assessment, structuring and lender alignment. Financing decisions and final terms remain subject to independent lender evaluation.
