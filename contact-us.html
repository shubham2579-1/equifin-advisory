Our Role

Clear Advice. Independent Perspective.

EquiFin Advisory is a corporate finance and debt advisory firm. We advise businesses on capital strategy, transaction structuring and financing execution.

We are not a lender and do not directly extend credit or guarantee financing approvals, interest rates or credit ratings.

Our role is to help clients evaluate and structure financing requirements in a manner aligned with their business, cash flows and transaction objectives.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I share before an initial discussion?

A brief overview of the business, the capital requirement, intended use of funds and approximate transaction timeline is generally sufficient for an initial discussion.

Do I need a detailed financial model before contacting EquiFin?

Not necessarily. Transaction readiness requirements vary. An initial discussion can help identify the information and analysis required for the next stage.

Does EquiFin directly provide loans?

No. EquiFin Advisory acts as an independent corporate finance and debt advisory firm and does not directly lend or extend credit.

Can EquiFin help with refinancing an existing facility?

Yes. Refinancing requirements can involve reviewing the existing debt structure, repayment profile, pricing, security arrangements and alignment with current business cash flows.

Does EquiFin work with businesses across India?

Yes. EquiFin supports businesses and transactions across India, subject to the nature and suitability of the advisory requirement.

Can I contact EquiFin for an early-stage financing discussion?

Yes. Where a transaction is still being evaluated, an initial discussion can help clarify capital requirements, transaction readiness and potential financing considerations.

Start With the Transaction Context.

A financing requirement is rarely just about the amount of capital required. The structure, repayment profile, security, cash-flow cycle and timing of the transaction can materially affect whether the capital supports the business effectively. If you are evaluating a financing requirement, refinancing an existing facility or planning a larger capital transaction, start with a conversation.
